# Quantifying Trader Intuition

### Quantitative Evaluation of the Predictive Value of Discretionary Trading Decisions

## Overview

This project investigates whether a discretionary trader's intuition provides measurable predictive value beyond a systematic, rule-based trading strategy.

The objective is to quantify subjective trading judgments, integrate them with objective market variables, and evaluate their incremental contribution to trade outcome prediction.

The project combines statistical modeling, machine learning, financial data analysis, and quantitative backtesting.

## Research Question

**Does discretionary trader intuition improve the predictive performance of a mechanical trading strategy, and under which market conditions does this improvement occur?**

## Research Objectives

- Develop a systematic multi-timeframe Ichimoku-based trading framework.
- Identify and quantify subjective trader judgment through structured features.
- Analyze interactions between discretionary intuition and objective market conditions.
- Compare mechanical and intuition-enhanced predictive models.
- Evaluate incremental predictive performance using rigorous statistical methods.
- Assess robustness through out-of-sample testing and backtesting.

## Methodology

The research follows five main stages:

1. **Data Exploration:** Analyze historical financial market data.
2. **Feature Engineering:** Construct objective market variables and subjective intuition indicators.
3. **Statistical Modeling:** Develop and compare predictive models.
4. **Model Evaluation:** Measure predictive performance and incremental value.
5. **Backtesting:** Evaluate trading performance while accounting for transaction costs.
